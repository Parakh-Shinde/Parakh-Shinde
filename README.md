## How I Build

- I keep claims tied to files, tests, reports, screenshots, or reproducible commands.
- I document limitations and assumptions instead of hiding them.
- I design offensive-security work only for isolated, authorized lab environments.
- I separate detection, AI enrichment, authorization logic, and response decisions.
- I write reports that answer: what happened, why it matters, evidence, ATT&CK mapping, and next steps.
